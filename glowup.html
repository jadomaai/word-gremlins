🪧 Signpost guide: what each conjunction tells the reader

ConjunctionIts jobIt tells the reader…
because, as, sinceA reason“Here comes the reason.”
although, even though, but, yetA surprise or opposite“Here comes something you wouldn’t expect.”
while, whilst, as, whenSame time“This is happening at the same moment.”
soA result“Here comes what happened because of it.”
until, before, after, onceTime order“Here’s when it happened.”
if, unlessOnly if…“This only happens if something else does.”
andAdding an idea“Here’s another idea that goes with it.”

Test it: read the first part, then ask “Does the next part really do what my signpost promised?”
